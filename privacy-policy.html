Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains, in simple words, what information TheDigitalWork may collect and how we use it.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Information you share

When you contact us, request a quote, or use a form on our website, you may share details such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, and project information.

2. How we use your information

We use this information to reply to you, understand your project, prepare estimates, provide our services, improve our website, and keep basic business records.

3. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us run our website or communicate with you, or when sharing is required by law. We aim to share only what is reasonably needed.

4. Cookies and third-party services

Our website or embedded third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for basic operation, analytics, or communication. Those services may handle information under their own privacy policies.

5. Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact forms.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide services, meet legal requirements, or resolve business matters. You can ask us to correct or delete information, subject to any records we must keep.

7. Children

Our services are for businesses and people who can legally enter into service agreements. Our website is not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and contact

If you want to ask about, correct, or request deletion of your information, email hello@thedigitalwork.in. We may need to keep some information where the law requires it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when our practices change. The latest version and date will be shown here.
